Bhaskar Roberts

Research Interests: cryptography and quantum information

CV

I design algorithms for quantum computers that provide distinctly quantum forms of security. I also study how to make proof systems and multiparty computation more efficient.

I’m doing a post-doc at NTT Research. I previously completed my PhD in computer science at Berkeley, where I was grateful to be advised by Sanjam Garg and Umesh Vazirani, and was supported by an NDSEG fellowship. As an undergrad at Princeton (class of 2019), I studied electrical engineering with a minor in applied math, and worked primarily with Mark Zhandry.
